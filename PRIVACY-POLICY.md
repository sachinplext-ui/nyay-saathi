# NYAY SAATHI — Privacy Notice (Prototype)

**Last updated:** 27 November 2026

## 1. Current status

NYAY SAATHI is currently a public educational website prototype. It does not provide user registration, login, user profiles, a case database, evidence/file uploads, a lawyer marketplace, or online filing. Dashboard cases, reminders, and document categories are fictional interface examples.

This notice describes the current prototype only. It is not a promise that future account or storage features are already available.

## 2. Information entered into the current prototype

- The application-draft form builds text in the browser for preview, copy, and print. It is not submitted to a NYAY SAATHI server by the current static generator.
- The rules-based assistant processes the question in the browser using bundled guidance. It is not a connected AI legal service.
- Do not enter real names, addresses, phone numbers, FIR/case numbers, identity numbers, passwords, financial information, or confidential documents. Use fictional examples only.

## 3. Hosting and technical data

The website is hosted as a static site on GitHub Pages. GitHub and the user's browser/network provider may process technical information needed to deliver the website, such as request metadata, IP address, browser/device details, and security logs, under their own policies and terms. NYAY SAATHI does not control those independent services.

## 4. Cookies and local storage

The prototype does not intentionally create a NYAY SAATHI account cookie or store case records in local storage. A service worker may cache static website assets to support basic offline loading. Browser and hosting infrastructure may use their own technical mechanisms.

## 5. Sharing

The current prototype does not provide a feature to submit application drafts or case documents to NYAY SAATHI. If a user copies or prints text, the browser, operating system, printer, or destination application may handle that content under its own settings and policies.

## 6. Children and age eligibility

The prototype is not designed to collect personal information from children. Do not submit personal or legal case information. Any future account service will require a separate age-eligibility, guardian-consent, privacy, and legal review before launch.

## 7. Security and retention

Because the prototype does not maintain user accounts or a case database, it has no NYAY SAATHI account-record retention schedule at this stage. Technical logs may be handled by hosting providers under their policies. Do not treat this prototype as a secure evidence vault.

## 8. Future changes

Before adding accounts, case storage, uploads, AI processing, analytics, or payments, the project must publish an updated notice explaining the specific data collected, purpose, legal basis, recipients/processors, retention, deletion process, security controls, user rights, and contact route. Those features must remain disabled until appropriate privacy, security, child-safety, and legal reviews are completed.

## 9. Contact and review

A responsible project contact and a process for privacy requests must be established before any personal-data collection is enabled. This prototype notice is informational and should be reviewed by qualified privacy/legal professionals before any production launch.

## 10. Not legal advice

NYAY SAATHI is not a law firm, government service, or emergency service and does not create an advocate-client relationship.
