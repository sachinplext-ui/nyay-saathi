# NYAY SAATHI — Madhya Pradesh Starter Knowledge Pack

**Status:** Initial source-grounded guide for prototype development. Not legal advice, not a complete law database, and not reviewed/certified by an advocate.
**Prepared:** 27 November 2026
**Coverage:** Selected official complaint, legal-aid and oversight routes in Madhya Pradesh.

## 1. How the assistant must use this pack

- Explain only the scope supported by the linked official source.
- Show the authority, exact source page and the date the source was checked.
- Do not label a general grievance portal as an FIR filing system, emergency service or court.
- Do not state that an authority has jurisdiction over a particular allegation without checking the relevant statute, subject and facts.
- Do not predict outcomes or declare any person guilty.
- Do not request real names, addresses, phone numbers, FIR numbers, identity documents or private case papers in this prototype. Use fictional placeholders only.
- For urgent danger, tell the user to seek help from a trusted adult and the appropriate emergency service; do not route emergencies to an ordinary grievance portal.

## 2. Madhya Pradesh CM Helpline 181

**Official pages**
- [CM Helpline — About](https://www.cmhelpline.mp.gov.in/About.aspx)
- [CM Helpline — FAQ](https://cmhelpline.mp.gov.in/faq.html)
- [Contact details](https://cmhelpline.mp.gov.in/ContactUs.aspx)

**What the official pages say**
- CM Helpline 181 is operated under the State's Public Service Management Department.
- The About page describes calls about government-scheme information, complaints, demands/suggestions, corruption-related calls, complaint status, and specified public services.
- The FAQ says 181 is toll-free, call hours are 7:00 AM to 11:00 PM, and complaints can be registered when related to the Madhya Pradesh State Government.
- The contact page lists a separate contact number for callers outside Madhya Pradesh.

**Safe explanation**
CM Helpline is a state-government grievance and information channel. It is not an emergency service, court, legal representation service, or universal substitute for a police complaint/FIR or a specialist statutory complaint route. The assistant should direct users to check the current official FAQ and contact page before relying on operating hours or service scope.

**Do not claim**
- Every police matter or private dispute can be decided by CM Helpline.
- A complaint will necessarily be accepted, resolved in the caller's favour, or resolved within a fixed period.
- Calling 181 is equivalent to lodging an FIR.

## 3. Free legal aid — Madhya Pradesh State Legal Services Authority

**Official pages**
- [MPSLSA — Legal Aid](https://madhyapradesh.nalsa.gov.in/legal-aid/)
- [MPSLSA — Directory](https://madhyapradesh.nalsa.gov.in/directory/)
- [MPSLSA — District and Tehsil legal-services activities](https://madhyapradesh.nalsa.gov.in/dlsa-and-tlsa-activities/)
- [MPSLSA — Officers and officials](https://madhyapradesh.nalsa.gov.in/officers-and-officials-of-state-authority/)

**What the official Legal Aid page says**
- Legal aid can include advocate representation, eligible litigation expenses, preparation of pleadings and appeals, drafting legal documents, and advice on legal matters.
- It identifies categories under Section 12 of the Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987, including members of Scheduled Castes/Scheduled Tribes, women and children, persons with disabilities, persons in custody, industrial workmen and certain disaster/atrocity victims.
- The page states an income ceiling of ₹2,00,000 under Section 12(h) for Madhya Pradesh. This amount must be rechecked against the current official source before being displayed as current; eligibility may also arise under other Section 12 categories.

**Safe explanation**
A person can contact the relevant District Legal Services Authority or MPSLSA to ask about eligibility and assistance. The assistant must not promise eligibility based on a short description. Use the official directory to locate the relevant district authority and verify current contact details.

**Do not claim**
- Everyone automatically qualifies for free legal aid.
- A specific person is eligible without checking the current statutory category and applicable rules.
- A listed phone number or officer name is guaranteed current unless rechecked.

## 4. Madhya Pradesh Lokayukta — scope and complaint filing

**Official pages**
- [Scope and jurisdiction](https://mplokayukt.nic.in/scope-and-jurisdiction)
- [Filing of complaints](https://mplokayukt.nic.in/complaints-filing)
- [FAQ](https://mplokayukt.nic.in/faq)

**What the official pages say**
- The scope page describes the public servants and functionaries covered by the Madhya Pradesh Lokayukt Evam Up-Lokayukt Adhiniyam, 1981, and distinguishes matters assigned to the Lokayukt and Up-Lokayukt.
- The page states limits and exclusions, including a five-year subject-matter limit and specified matters already covered by other inquiry processes. Because the source page was last updated in 2020, these details must be checked against the current Act and amendments before being treated as current law.
- The complaint-filing page describes signed complaints, prescribed forms and affidavit requirements that differ depending on the category of public servant and whether an affidavit is directed.
- The same page describes a ₹25 judicial-stamp fee for complaints against a Minister or Secretary, and says a fee for other categories is required only if directed by the Lokayukt or Up-Lokayukt. The page was last updated 18 May 2022; verify current rules and fees before use.
- The FAQ says a complaint may be presented to the Secretary or sent by registered post and describes who may complain.

**Safe explanation**
Lokayukta is a specialist statutory inquiry route for matters within its legal jurisdiction concerning covered public servants. It is not the correct forum for every service grievance, private dispute, police issue or allegation against every person. The assistant should check the current Act, exact public-servant category, limitation, exclusions, forms and filing rules before giving procedural information.

**Do not claim**
- Every public employee, private person or advocate falls within Lokayukta jurisdiction.
- A complaint automatically starts an inquiry or proves misconduct.
- The old page's fee/form details are guaranteed current.

## 5. Advocate conduct — State Bar Council of Madhya Pradesh

**Official pages**
- [State Bar Council of Madhya Pradesh](https://sbcofmp.org.in/main.html)
- [General Rules of State Bar Council of MP](https://www.sbcofmp.org.in/Gen%20Rules%20SBC.htm)
- [Bar Council of India — State Bar Council directory](https://www.barcouncilofindia.org/info/sbc)

**What the rules page supports**
The State Bar Council rules page includes a Disciplinary Committee chapter and says the committee performs functions assigned under the Advocates Act and Bar Council of India rules.

**Safe explanation**
For a concern about an advocate's professional conduct, first verify the current complaint forum, procedure, form, fee and required documents with the State Bar Council and applicable Advocates Act/BCI rules. The rules page alone is not a complete step-by-step complaint guide.

**Do not claim**
- The website's rules page establishes that a specific advocate committed misconduct.
- The same complaint process applies to every state or every type of dispute.
- A fee or filing requirement without checking current official instructions.

## 9. Additional primary-source checks (27 November 2026)

### 9.1 Legal-aid income ceiling cross-check

The current NALSA Legal Aid and FAQ pages both list the Madhya Pradesh annual income ceiling under Section 12(h) as **₹2,00,000** for cases before courts other than the Supreme Court. NALSA separately states the Supreme Court income ceiling as ₹5,00,000. The statutory Section 12 categories remain separate eligibility routes; the income ceiling is not the only way a person may qualify.

- [NALSA — Legal Aid](https://nalsa.gov.in/legal-aid/)
- [NALSA — FAQs](https://nalsa.gov.in/faqs/)
- [MPSLSA — Legal Aid](https://madhyapradesh.nalsa.gov.in/legal-aid/)

**Implementation rule:** Show the amount only with the jurisdiction, annual basis, court-level qualification and “verify current rules” note. Do not infer a person's eligibility from income alone or ask a user to disclose income in this public demo. Source pages checked 27 November 2026.

### 9.2 Lokayukta Act — primary-source location

The official India Code file search surfaced a Government-hosted copy titled **The Madhya Pradesh Act No. 37 of 1981**, the Madhya Pradesh Lokayukt Evam Up-Lokayukt Adhiniyam, 1981:

- [India Code — MP Act No. 37 of 1981 PDF](https://upload.indiacode.nic.in/showfile?actid=AC_MP_74_256_00002_00002_1540466798580&filename=Adhiniyam1981.pdf&type=actfile)

The located document identifies the Act as applying to the whole State of Madhya Pradesh and describes its purpose as providing authorities to enquire into allegations against public servants. It is an Act text copy, but this check did **not** establish a consolidated, fully amended current version or confirm every current rule, limitation, exclusion, form or fee.

**Implementation rule:** Use the PDF as a primary-source starting point only. Before explaining present-day filing eligibility or procedure, verify subsequent amending Acts, rules and Gazette notifications. Do not treat the old Lokayukta website fee/form information as current solely because it remains online. No conclusion about any individual complaint's jurisdiction should be generated from this entry alone.

### 9.3 MP Police citizen portal — limited official description

The official Indore Police Commissionerate page links to the Madhya Pradesh Police Citizen Portal and describes it generally as a platform for citizen police services:

- [Indore Police Commissionerate — official portal page](https://indore.mppolice.gov.in/)

This page does not, by itself, establish the current workflow or legal effect of every portal service. The starter pack has not verified a complete service-by-service catalogue.

**Implementation rule:** Link users to the official portal for current service descriptions. Never state that a portal submission is an FIR, that a particular service is available in every district, or that an online submission guarantees police action unless the exact official service page confirms it.
