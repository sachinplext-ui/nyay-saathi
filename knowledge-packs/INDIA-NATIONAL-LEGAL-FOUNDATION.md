# NYAY SAATHI — National Legal Foundation (India)

**Status:** Research foundation for the Pan-India prototype; not a complete legal database and not legal advice.  
**Research date:** 28 November 2026  
**Coverage:** Selected central primary-law sources and national legal-aid source. State/UT procedure is maintained separately.

## 1. Purpose

This document records a small, source-grounded national starting set that can be shared across State and Union Territory experiences. It does not establish that every provision applies identically in every territory or to every historical event. The exact law in force, commencement, amendments, territorial exceptions and saving/transition provisions must be checked for each answer.

## 2. Core criminal-law enactments

### 2.1 Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 (Act 45 of 2023)

- Official India Code record: https://www.indiacode.nic.in/indiacode/handle/123456789/20062?view_type=search
- Official Act PDF: https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/20062/1/a202345.pdf
- Official commencement notification (S.O. 850(E), 23 February 2024): https://www.mha.gov.in/sites/default/files/BhartiyaNyayaSanhita_24022024.pdf
- India Code records enactment on 25 December 2023 and enforcement date 1 July 2024.
- The commencement notification appoints 1 July 2024, except sub-section (2) of section 106.

**Implementation caution:** Do not assume that a provision excluded from commencement is in force. Check later Gazette notifications and amendments before answering. For conduct before commencement or pending proceedings, inspect repeal/savings and transition provisions and obtain qualified legal review where needed.

### 2.2 Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023 (Act 46 of 2023)

- Official India Code record: https://www.indiacode.nic.in/indiacode/handle/123456789/20099?view_type=browse
- Official commencement notification (S.O. 848(E), 23 February 2024): https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2024/252354.pdf
- India Code records enforcement date 1 July 2024.
- Section 1 contains a territorial qualification for Nagaland and specified tribal areas, subject to possible State notification and statutory qualifications.

**Implementation caution:** The territorial wording must be checked against applicable State notifications and the precise subject. Do not assume uniform application without this check. For earlier events or pending proceedings, verify the relevant savings/transition provisions.

### 2.3 Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023 (Act 47 of 2023)

- Official India Code record: https://www.indiacode.nic.in/indiacode/handle/123456789/20063?view_type=search
- India Code records enactment on 25 December 2023 and enforcement date 1 July 2024.
- Section 1 describes its application to judicial proceedings, with stated exclusions for affidavits and arbitration proceedings.

**Implementation caution:** Before answering a specific evidence question, retrieve the exact current provision, amendments and any applicable transition/savings rule. Do not provide a conclusion about admissibility based only on this index entry.

## 3. National legal-aid foundation

### National Legal Services Authority (NALSA)

- Official Legal Aid page: https://nalsa.gov.in/legal-aid/
- Official FAQ: https://nalsa.gov.in/faqs/
- NALSA describes eligibility categories under section 12 of the Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987, including women and children, Scheduled Castes/Scheduled Tribes, persons with disabilities, persons in custody and other listed categories.

**Implementation caution:** Do not promise that a particular person qualifies. Check the current Act, applicable rules, income criteria where relevant, and the State/District Legal Services Authority process. A national page does not replace a verified local referral.

## 4. National source record format

Every future legal-source record should contain:

- source ID and exact title;
- issuing authority and jurisdiction;
- official URL to the exact Act, Gazette notification, judgment or service page;
- instrument type and section/rule/paragraph;
- enactment/publication date and commencement/effective date;
- amendment, repeal and saving/transition status;
- territorial scope and exceptions;
- retrieval/last-checked date;
- source language and version;
- verification status and reviewer.

A portal homepage alone is not evidence for a legal proposition. Prefer the exact primary instrument or notification. Do not mark a source "legal_reviewed" until a qualified Indian legal professional has reviewed it.

## 5. Product rules for Pan-India answers

1. Ask for State/UT only when it materially changes the answer; do not ask for identifying details.
2. Separate Central law from State/UT law and local administrative procedure.
3. Determine the relevant date before mapping provisions where commencement or transition may matter.
4. Retrieve the exact provision and any commencement/amendment notification.
5. Display the issuing authority, exact source link, provision and last-checked date.
6. If territorial application, currentness or legal effect is uncertain, say so and do not guess.
7. Never promise an FIR, bail, arrest, legal-aid eligibility, court result or authority action.
8. Use synthetic test cases only. Do not collect real FIRs, identity documents, private case papers, addresses or contact details in this prototype.
9. Do not enable a public legal-answer AI until retrieval, citation accuracy, privacy/security review, synthetic QA and qualified legal review are complete.

## 6. Verification boundary

This document records official source locations and a limited set of metadata checked on 28 November 2026. It is **not** a consolidated current-law digest, a complete amendment history, a comprehensive territory analysis or an advocate-reviewed guide. All state/UT directories remain separate and must be researched individually.
