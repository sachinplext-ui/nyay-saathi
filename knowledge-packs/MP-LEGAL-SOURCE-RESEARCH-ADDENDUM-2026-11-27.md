## 1. Madhya Pradesh Lokayukta Act — what the Government-hosted copy shows

Primary source: [India Code — The Madhya Pradesh Act No. 37 of 1981 (PDF)](https://upload.indiacode.nic.in/showfile?actid=AC_MP_74_256_00002_00002_1540466798580&filename=Adhiniyam1981.pdf&type=actfile).

The PDF identifies the Madhya Pradesh Lokayukt Evam Up-Lokayukt Adhiniyam, 1981 and says it extends to the whole State. Its stated purpose concerns inquiry into allegations against public servants. The copy includes amendment footnotes referencing, among others, amendments from 1982, 1985, 1986, 1998 and 2003. This is not proof that it is a consolidated version containing every later amendment or current subordinate rule.

The PDF's printed Section 9 describes a prescribed complaint form and affidavit, and a ₹25 deposit with exceptions and discretion described in the provisos. Because the accessible copy's consolidation/currentness has not been established, NYAY SAATHI must **not** display ₹25 as a current filing fee or give the old form/affidavit text as current instructions. The same section includes a provision concerning wilfully or maliciously false complaints; the assistant must not casually characterize a disputed or unsuccessful complaint as malicious.

The PDF's printed Section 10 says the Lokayukt/Up-Lokayukt decides the inquiry procedure while ensuring natural justice. Section 12 describes written findings/recommendations to the competent authority and a response on action taken or proposed. These are descriptions of the text in the located copy, not a guarantee about any individual matter or present-day filing route.

**Implementation status:** Keep current filing fee, form, affidavit, limitation, exclusions and detailed eligibility marked **unverified pending Gazette/amendment/rules review**. Do not give individualized jurisdiction conclusions from this copy alone.

## 2. Advocate professional-misconduct complaints

Sources:
- [Advocates Act framework discussed in Madhya Pradesh High Court judgment (5 October 2021)](https://mphc.gov.in/upload/indore/MPHCIND/2021/WP/18183/WP_18183_2021_FinalOrder_05-Oct-2021.pdf)
- [State Bar Council of Madhya Pradesh — General Rules](https://www.sbcofmp.org.in/Gen%20Rules%20SBC.htm)

The High Court judgment quotes Section 6(1)(c) of the Advocates Act as assigning State Bar Councils the function of entertaining and determining misconduct cases against advocates on their roll. It quotes Section 35(1): where the State Bar Council receives a complaint or otherwise has reason to believe an advocate on its roll is guilty of professional or other misconduct, it refers the case for disposal to its Disciplinary Committee. The quoted Section 35(2) describes notice/hearing steps.

The State Bar Council's published General Rules identify one or more Disciplinary Committees, provide for a register of complaints against advocates and action taken, and say the Disciplinary Committee performs functions assigned under the Act and Bar Council of India rules.

**Limits:** The published rules page does not provide a complete current public-facing complaint checklist, current fee, prescribed complaint form, filing address or expected timeline. The cited High Court judgment is from 2021 and is not a substitute for checking the current Act, BCI rules and Council instructions. NYAY SAATHI should explain the general statutory route only and direct users to verify current filing requirements with the State Bar Council. It must not infer misconduct from a one-sided allegation.
