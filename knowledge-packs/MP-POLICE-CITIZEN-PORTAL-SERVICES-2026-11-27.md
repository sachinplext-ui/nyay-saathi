# MP Police Citizen Portal — Service Catalogue Research Note
**Checked:** 27 November 2026  
**Purpose:** NYAY SAATHI development reference only. This is not legal advice and does not guarantee that a service is available for every district, user, or type of matter.

## 1. Official Indore Police citizen-services menu

The official Commissionerate of Police Indore website displays the following categories and menu items:

- **Report Online:** Complaint Lodging; Lost Article Report; Information to Police.
- **Verification:** Character Verification; Tenant Verification; Domestic Help.
- **Information About:** Missing Person; Stolen/Lost Vehicle; Recovered Vehicle.
- **Citizen Information:** View FIR; Arrested Person; Passport Status; Indore Traffic Saathi app.

Source: [Commissionerate of Police Indore — official website](https://indore.mppolice.gov.in/).

This is a menu listing, not a full procedure guide. The exact form fields, eligibility, documents, district coverage, processing time and legal effect of each submission were not established by this check.

## 2. Government district-service description

The Sehore district website's page for the Madhya Pradesh Police Citizen Portal describes these functions:
- Giving police information about lost property (such as mobile phones, SIM cards and documents) and receiving an acknowledgement.
- Reporting incidents and suspicious activities, including a facility described as not disclosing the informant's identity.
- Citizen feedback/interactions with police.
- Information about missing persons and unidentified dead bodies.
- Searching seized/stolen vehicles using engine, chassis or registration details.

Source: [District Sehore, Government of Madhya Pradesh — MP Police Citizen Portal](https://sehore.nic.in/en/service/madhya-pradesh-police-citizen-portal/).

Treat this as the district page's description, not a guarantee of anonymity, confidentiality, acceptance, investigation or outcome. Do not encourage users to submit sensitive or identifying details through this prototype.

## 3. FIR information is not the same as filing an FIR

The Indore Police FIR Status page says the MP Police Citizen Portal provides information about FIRs of crimes registered within Madhya Pradesh and describes registration/login using OTP.

Source: [Indore Police — FIR Status](https://indore.mppolice.gov.in/fir-status/).

The Indore website also lists **View FIR** under Citizen Information. These sources support describing an online facility to view or obtain information about registered FIRs. They do **not**, by themselves, establish that every complaint-lodging form is an FIR, or that submitting a portal complaint automatically registers an FIR.

## 4. State-level confirmation of citizen-services platform

A Madhya Pradesh Government information document published by MPINFO says CCTNS citizen services can be accessed through the state citizen portal, MP Police website and MP e-Cop app. It refers to a Home Department communication dated 10 December 2025.

Source: [MPINFO — Government information document (17 February 2026)](https://www.mpinfo.org/mpinfoNew/Uploads/PDF/Tuesday%2C%20February%2017%2C%202026/D-170226020441.pdf).

## 5. Safe product wording and restrictions

NYAY SAATHI may say:
- “The Indore Police website lists online complaint lodging, lost-article reporting and information-to-police options.”
- “The MP Police portal is described by a state district website as supporting lost-property information/acknowledgement, police information, citizen feedback and selected missing-person/vehicle information.”
- “The Indore Police website lists a facility to view information about registered FIRs.”

NYAY SAATHI must not say:
- “Every online complaint is an FIR.”
- “A portal submission guarantees police action, FIR registration or a particular result.”
- “Anonymous reporting guarantees anonymity or confidentiality.”
- “Every listed service is available in every district with identical procedure.”
- “The portal replaces emergency assistance or direct contact with the appropriate police authority.”

For urgent danger, direct users to seek immediate help from a trusted adult and the appropriate emergency service. Do not request real FIR copies, identity documents, addresses, phone numbers or other sensitive personal information in the prototype; use fictional examples only.
